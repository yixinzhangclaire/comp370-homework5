# Complaint Type Analysis

For January and February 2024, the most common complaint type was `HEAT/HOT WATER`, with a total of 81,631 complaints across all boroughs.

The borough-level counts for January-February were:

- Bronx: 30,139
- Brooklyn: 20,915
- Manhattan: 17,604
- Queens: 12,107
- Staten Island: 866

For June and July 2024, the same complaint type had only 6,889 complaints:

- Bronx: 2,227
- Brooklyn: 1,929
- Manhattan: 1,777
- Queens: 835
- Staten Island: 121

Overall, `HEAT/HOT WATER` complaints decreased by 74,742 cases, which is approximately a 91.56% decrease from January-February to June-July.

This suggests a strong seasonal pattern. Heating and hot-water complaints are much more common during the colder winter months and decrease substantially during the summer.
